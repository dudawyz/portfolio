Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before we start working together.

How do we get started?
We begin with a quick chat about what you need. From there, we agree on the scope of work and I start supporting your tasks remotely — usually within a few days.
Do you work hourly or per project?
Both. Hourly support works well for ongoing tasks, while per-project rates suit one-off work like a research batch or a content project. We'll choose whatever fits your workload best.
What are your working hours and time zones?
I work flexibly across European and US-friendly hours and coordinate around your time zone. We'll agree on response times and availability up front, so expectations are always clear.
How do we communicate?
Whatever works best for you — email, a shared task tool, or scheduled check-in calls. I keep communication proactive and clear, so you're never left wondering about the status of a task.
Is my information kept confidential?
Absolutely. Discretion and confidentiality are central to how I work. Any access I'm given to your accounts or information is handled with care, and I'm always happy to sign an NDA.
What if I need more hours than expected?
No problem — we can simply add more hours as needed. You'll always be kept informed before any extra time is used, so there are never surprises.
Which languages do you work in?
I work in English and Portuguese, and handle all administrative communication in English.

Still have a question?

Reach out directly — I'm happy to talk through anything before we begin.
